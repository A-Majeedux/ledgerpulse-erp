Kind decides where an account can be picked:
  • Bank Cash the only accounts offered as “Paid from” on a vendor payment and “Deposit to” on a customer receipt. On a journal entry they are picked in the General account column.
  • Trade receivable Trade payable the two accounts all customers and all vendors share. On a journal entry you pick the customer or vendor by name, and the line lands here.
  • Non-trading receivable Non-trading payable one account per person outside normal trade, such as a director or an employee. Picked in the Partner account column of a journal entry.
  • General everything else: revenue, expenses, tax, equity, other assets and liabilities. Picked in the General account column of a journal entry.
